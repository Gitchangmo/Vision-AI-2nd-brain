# Excerpts

- "Area scan cameras capture a full image all at once and are commonly used for
  part presence and code reading. Line scan cameras build an image one line at
  a time as the target moves, which makes them a good fit for continuous
  materials or fast-moving parts."
- "Faster production lines require higher frame rates and shorter exposure
  times to avoid motion blur. Shorter exposure reduces blur but also limits
  available light, so lighting must be properly configured."
- "A high-resolution industrial machine vision camera cannot recover any detail
  that the lens cannot resolve."
- "A frequent error is prioritizing resolution over environmental factors, as
  high pixel counts cannot compensate for poor lighting or optical issues."
- "Additionally, relying on automatic settings often introduces instability,
  particularly in color inspections where shifting white balance can
  compromise results."
- Step 1 (Selecting Based on Resolution):
  - "Detection capability = 4 pixel area"
  - "Dimensional tolerance = ±5 pixels"
  - "Based on these calculations, if the inspection requires detection of
    foreign particles that are as small as 0.1 mm 0.004″ with a field of view
    of 30 mm 1.18″ , a camera with a resolution of 2MP or more is needed."
- Step 2 (Color or Monochrome):
  - "Generally speaking, if the differences at the sensing points are detected
    based on hue, the color cameras may have an advantage."
